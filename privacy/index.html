Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

This site does not collect, store, sell or share personal information. It sets no cookies, keeps nothing in your browser’s storage, runs no analytics or advertising, and loads nothing from other companies’ servers. The live demo runs entirely on your device.

Who runs this site

This website, iyiolacak.github.io/ai-ui-rewrite-rust, is the project page of the open-source ai-ui-rewrite-rust project and is run by its maintainer (“we”, “us”). This policy covers this website only.

What we collect

Nothing. The site has no accounts, sign-up or contact forms, comments or newsletter. It uses no cookies, local storage, session storage or similar technologies, and no analytics, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or advertising. Its pages, fonts, images and scripts are all served from this site; there are no embedded videos, social widgets or third-party scripts. The only personal information we ever receive is what you choose to send us by email (see Contact).

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. As with any website, your browser has to send your IP address and ordinary request details (such as your browser type and the page requested) to the server to load a page. GitHub states that it logs and stores the IP address of visitors to GitHub Pages sites for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. GitHub handles that information under its own privacy statement. We do not have access to those logs and receive no visitor information or statistics from GitHub.

The live demo

The demo is a program compiled to WebAssembly that runs inside your browser tab. Its replies come from a demonstration model built into the program, not from an online AI service. The conversation history is generated on your device, and anything you type into the demo stays in that tab: it is never sent to us or anyone else, and it is gone when you reload or close the page. The demo downloads only its own files from this site. If you deliberately add a ?thread= address to the page’s URL, your browser fetches the file at the address you entered, from whichever server hosts it.

Links to other websites

This site links to other websites, such as GitHub, assistant-ui and Slint. When you follow a link you leave this site, and the other website’s own privacy policy applies.

Children

This site is a technical project page for software developers and is not directed at children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the EU and UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights over personal information an organisation holds about you, including the rights to access, correct and delete it, and to object to its use. We hold no personal information about visitors to this site. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to show you or delete that correspondence. We do not sell or share personal information. For information GitHub holds about your visit, contact GitHub as described in its privacy statement. You are still welcome to contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If the site changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

Contact

For questions about this policy or privacy requests, email mogulbagul@gmail.com. We use what you send only to reply to you, and delete it once the matter is closed.

For general questions you can also open an issue on the project’s GitHub repository. Issues are public, so please don’t include personal details there. Whatever you post on GitHub is handled by GitHub under its privacy statement.
